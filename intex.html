🏰 A Royal History

The British monarchy has a long and interesting history. Over many centuries, kings and queens have played an important role in the history and culture of Britain. The monarchy has changed greatly over time and continues to exist today as a constitutional monarchy.

👑 Important Monarchs

Queen Victoria

Queen Victoria became queen in 1837. Her reign lasted more than 63 years and the period became known as the Victorian era.

King George VI

George VI became king in 1936. He was the father of Queen Elizabeth II and was monarch during much of the Second World War.

Queen Elizabeth II

Elizabeth II became queen in 1952. She served as monarch for more than 70 years and became the longest-reigning British monarch.

King Charles III

Charles III became king in 2022 after the death of his mother, Queen Elizabeth II.

🌸 Queen Elizabeth II

Queen Elizabeth II was born in 1926 and became queen in 1952. During her long reign, she met many world leaders and witnessed major changes in British society and around the world. She was the longest-reigning British monarch. She died in September 2022 after more than seven decades on the throne.

💙 Princess Diana

Princess Diana became a member of the British Royal Family when she married Charles, Prince of Wales, in 1981. She became internationally famous for her style and, especially, for her charity work. She supported many charitable causes and helped bring attention to humanitarian issues. Diana remains one of the most remembered members of the modern British Royal Family.
